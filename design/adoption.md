# Application validation and maintenance ownership

Release preparation covers the evaluation procedure, reusable evidence record,
shared real-raster regression corpus, executed automated rehearsals and maintenance
plan. Independent adopter validation follows organically after release. Collect
application reports as users arrive, without a recruitment target or a requirement
to obtain production usage or case studies before release.

Independent adoption is unqualified. No independent application, production
retention result, consented case study, or funded support agreement is recorded
here. Repository rehearsals establish only their recorded artifacts and scopes.
The public [evaluation procedure](../docs/how-to/evaluate.md) and
[record template](../docs/examples/application-evaluation.md) collect evidence
without treating prepared CI or automated examples as adopter outcomes.

## Priority decisions

At each review, list observed obstacles by application and artifact, distinguish
reproduction from conjecture, and link each recurring obstacle to a focused issue.
Order work by fidelity/data-loss/recovery impact, blocked integration, recurrence,
then implementation and ongoing maintenance cost. Record a decision and next
review date, including a decision to defer. Absence of adopter evidence leaves
priorities provisional.

Broad Zarr/xarray support or a full catalog platform requires an actual application,
request/access patterns, the deficiency of existing paths, a bounded acceptance
test, and someone responsible for ongoing qualification. Sponsorship can fund
maintenance or a focused fix. Paid support needs defined contracts and backup
coverage; managed hosting additionally needs observed demand, costs, isolation,
recovery and staffing. Do not commit to either service from local timings.
